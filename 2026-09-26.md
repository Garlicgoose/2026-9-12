# Sequence

## First

- The balance of the PO items ==appear== to [be accounted for under]{在……项下能够对应上} subsequent tracking/deliveries and there ==are== no other POs that are open for this PCI location site as verified on the Meta report.

>The balance **appear** to be accounted for and there **are** no other POs
>剩余部分似乎已经能够对应上,而且没有其他PO

>of the PO items --> balance
>under subsequent tracking/deliveried --> be accounted for

>[The Attributive Clause]{定语从句}: that **are** open --> POs
>处于Open状态的PO
>for this PCI location site --> POs
>这个PCI site中的

>as verified on the Meta report --> and ...
>正如 Meta report所核实的那样. 用于修饰前面这一件事

> [!note]- Structure
> ```
> 第一大句
> The balance appears to be accounted for
> │
> ├─ The balance
> │    └─ of the PO items
> │
> └─ appears
>      └─ to be accounted for
>           └─ under subsequent tracking/deliveries
> 
> 
>                     AND
> 
> 
> 第二大句
> There are no other POs
> │
> └─ POs
>      └─ that are open

## as + 过去分词

一种压缩表达,某些**从句的主语和 be 动词**在意思足够清楚的时候，可以省略.

- as (if was) verified on the report

其翻译不一定是正如...
还可以是根据.. \ as requested (按照你的要求) \ 

### 后接介词

1. by + sb
2. in + sth
3. below / above : 如下\如上
4. time: today / last week

### As

1. as = 正如
2. as = 当……的时候 / 随着 (这个时候要接完整的句子)
	> As I was checking the invoice, I found an error.
3. as = 因为 / 由于 (类似because)
4. as = 作为 (后接名词)
	> He works as a logistics coordinator.

that 作为宾语从句的连接词时,可以省略


## 常用写法

| 你想表达              | 推荐英文                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 由于操作错误            | **Due to an error**                            |
| 本次付款包含            | **This payment includes...**                   |
| 重复付款              | **duplicate payment**                          |
| 重复付款金额            | **duplicate amount**                           |
| 附件中的其他 Sheet      | **the other sheets in the attached file**      |
| 相关 Invoice Number | **the related invoice numbers**                |
| 开具 DN             | **issue a DN**                                 |
| 开具 CN             | **issue a CN**                                 |
| 在下次付款中抵扣          | **offset the amount against the next payment** |
| 供你参考              | **for your reference**                         |

Due to an error, this payment includes a duplicate payment of HKD 3,860,867.48.
Please refer to the other sheets in the attachment for the invoice numbers related to the duplicate payment.
We will issue a DN to offset the duplicate amount against the next payment.Alternatively, you may provide us with a CN.

due to 是一个介词短语,从句一般需要主谓

## of & for

of: 表示从属关系
>the amount of the payment
>the date of the invoice

for: 用途\对象\目的\受益者
>this file is for your reference
>the DN is for the next payment

for还可以作为连词,接完整的句子
> I didn't go outside, for is was rainning.

## 何时使用the?

the: 那个/这些我们知道的特指

an invoice 不知道哪张
the invoice 那张特定的发票

第一次出现的时候,一般使用 a/ an
第二次开始就可以使用the,因为前面提到了,默认双方已知
